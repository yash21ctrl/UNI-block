# CORE_APP_FLOW.md (The Absolute Workflow)

## Roles
- **ROLE 1: FIELD J.E. (Junior Engineer)**
- **ROLE 2: SECTION CONTROLLER (Cockpit)**
- **ROLE 3: STATION MASTER (SM Terminal)**

---

## The Exact 5-Step Process

### STEP 1: THE REQUEST (Field JE)
- Field JE opens their portal and requests a block (Selects track/junction, reason, and requested duration).
- JE submits and is redirected to a "Waiting for Sanction" page.
- On this page, a QR code is displayed but it is **LOCKED/BLURRED**. The JE cannot do anything yet.

### STEP 2: THE SANCTION (Controller Cockpit)
- Controller sees the request in the Cockpit.
- Controller uses the AI/Pareto to schedule and sanction the block.
- Once sanctioned, the Official Memo is sent to the nearest Station Master AND the Field JE.
- The QR code on the Field JE's portal **instantly UNLOCKS**.

### STEP 3: GROUND VERIFICATION (Station Master & JE)
- Field JE shows the Unlocked QR Code to the Station Master.
- Station Master uses their terminal scanner to scan the JE's QR code.
- The Station Master now has **TWO choices**:

#### [PATH 3A: DISAGREE / GROUND HAZARD]
- If there is an emergency (storm/train delay), SM clicks "Reject/Defer".
- SM **MUST enter their ID and Password** to authorize the rejection, along with the reason.
- Alert goes instantly to the Cockpit.
- The AI automatically finds the **NEXT BEST SLOT** for the block, updates the Gantt chart, and sends updated Memos with new timings to both the SM and JE.

#### [PATH 3B: AGREE / GRANT BLOCK]
- SM clicks "Grant Disconnection".
- The block is officially active.

### STEP 4: EXECUTION & TIMER (Field JE)
- Once the SM accepts, the Field JE's portal automatically moves to the "Execution Page".
- JE **must upload a "Before / Defect Photo"**.
- Once uploaded, a **Live Countdown Timer begins**, showing the time left for the block.
- JE starts working on the track.

### STEP 5: COMPLETION & PROOF (Field JE & Controller)
- When work is done, JE clicks "Work Done".
- JE is prompted to upload an **"After / Finished Photo"**.
- JE submits. The block is closed and track is surrendered.
- In the Controller Cockpit, the Controller clicks on the completed block and **can see BOTH the "Before Photo" and "After Photo"** as proof of execution.
